# Asset attribution

This fork no longer ships the office scene: the LimeZu tilesets (`tilesets/`), the Tiled maps
(`maps/`) and the procedurally drawn cast portraits (`scene/office/portraitArt.ts`) were removed
together with it, so no third-party pixel art is bundled any more.

The bundled fonts carry their own licence in `fonts/LICENSE.txt`.

## Icons (Lucide, ISC)
